## Summary of what to prioritize
1. **Product images are completely missing everywhere** (catalogue cards, product detail page, related products, cart line items) — every product renders a static "нет фото" placeholder instead of `product.images[0]`. This dwarfs every other gap; `content/products.ts` / `lib/catalogue.ts` should already have image data to wire up (not checked in this pass — image URLs weren't verified to exist in the migrated data layer).
2. The shared `Dialog.tsx` primitive is a good architectural call (fixes real a11y gaps the original never had — no `role="dialog"`/focus trap there) but its visual/motion language (plain 150-180ms CSS ease) doesn't match the original's spring-driven (`stiffness 260, damping 28`) sheet and the original's docked-desktop/bottom-sheet-mobile split is gone.
3. No `motion` package anywhere in migrated — every hover/press micro-interaction (gradient direction shifts, whileTap scale, animated glyph colors on the qty stepper, radial-gradient cart icon) was flattened to plain Tailwind `transition` classes or dropped outright.
4. `PriceQtyButton` (qty+running-total pill, the actual primary CTA on the original product page) has no migrated equivalent at all — the migrated product page uses the generic grid-card `AddToCartButton` instead.
5. Possible live bug (unverified due to sandbox limitations): the `CartDrawer` panel may render permanently off-screen (stuck at the slide-in animation's 0% frame) — `app/globals.css:30-37` itself documents this having been seen before at 430px; recommend a manual check in a real browser at both mobile and desktop widths.
